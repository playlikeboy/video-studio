# Video design systems

A design system is a versioned set of tokens and rules, not a random list of effects.

## Required tokens
- canvas dimensions and aspect ratio
- color palette
- typography families and scale
- spacing and safe margins
- grid and alignment
- shapes and corner radii
- caption style
- transition language
- animation duration/easing/stagger
- shadow, blur and grain
- logo and end-card rules
- audio palette

Use one primary and one secondary typeface unless the preset explicitly allows more. Maintain contrast and safe zones. Use motion to guide attention. Keep caption and title hierarchy consistent. Verify font script coverage and license.
