# Storytelling and editorial decisions

## Narrative structure
For short-form videos, consider hook, context, development, payoff and close/call to action. Not every video needs all five. Follow source material and creator intent.

## Shot selection
- Prefer clear, relevant shots.
- Preserve continuity and screen direction where it matters.
- Avoid repeated shots unless intentional.
- Use B-roll that genuinely supports the spoken sentence.
- Do not fabricate events or visuals that misrepresent the source.
- Avoid cutting on every beat; use musical rhythm as support, not the whole story.

## Talking-head editing
Remove clear recording mistakes and dead air. Preserve natural pauses, breaths and emotional beats. Use punch-ins sparingly and vary framing with purpose. Do not insert stock B-roll that implies facts not present in narration. Review transcript decisions before destructive edits.

## Reference videos
Analyze pacing, framing, typography, transitions and overall design language. Use as inspiration; do not reproduce copyrighted source footage, distinctive branding or exact proprietary artwork.
